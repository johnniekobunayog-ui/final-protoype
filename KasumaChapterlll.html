KABANATA III
ANG INIWANG KUBO

After surviving the terrifying encounters deep within the forest, Elias and his companions finally discovered an old wooden hut hidden beyond a narrow trail. Exhausted and with nowhere else to go, they decided to take shelter inside. The moment they stepped through the doorway, an unsettling silence filled the room. Dust-covered furniture, faded photographs, and abandoned belongings suggested that someone had once lived there. Yet something felt terribly wrong. The hut looked abandoned, but it did not feel empty. While searching the rooms, Elias discovered an old diary beneath a pile of broken belongings. Its pages contained accounts of missing villagers, strange figures seen wandering outside at night, and warnings about something that had been living among them.

One entry caught his attention. It mentioned a series of unexplained disappearances and a family that had vanished without leaving a trace. The final page contained only one sentence: "Huwag na huwag mong bubuksan ang pinto kapag narinig mo ang sarili mong pangalan." Suddenly, a loud thud came from the upper floor. Everyone froze. Mateo insisted that nobody had gone upstairs. But as Elias turned toward the staircase, he noticed a shadow slowly disappearing at the end of the hallway.

Then came three slow knocks from the front door. Nobody answered.

A familiar voice spoke from the other side. It sounded exactly like someone they had left behind in the forest.

But there was one problem. That person was already standing inside the hut.

“May mga lihim na nakabaon sa loob ng kubo. At may mga nilalang na hindi kailanman umalis.”

PHILIPPINES — 1998
