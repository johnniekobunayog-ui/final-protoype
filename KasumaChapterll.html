KABANATA II
ANG BULONG NG KAGUBATAN

The celebration had ended. Elias and his companions began their journey home through an old forest trail, hoping to reach their village before midnight.

As they walked deeper into the woods, the sounds of the fiesta slowly disappeared. The only things they could hear were the rustling leaves and the occasional footsteps behind them.
Suddenly, one of his companions stopped. Something was watching them.

A strange figure appeared between the trees. Its movements were unnatural, and its presence filled the forest with an overwhelming sense of fear. They realized that it was not an ordinary creature. An Aswang.

With nowhere to run, they searched for a place to hide. They would need to explore their surroundings, find useful supplies, and discover a way to escape before the creature found them.

But the forest held more secrets than they could imagine.

“Sa gubat, hindi lahat ng naririnig mo ay hangin. At hindi lahat ng sumusunod sa'yo ay tao.”

PHILIPPINES — 1998
